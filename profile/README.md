## About us

WPGenius Solutions LLP is a WordPress development studio building custom themes, plugins, learning platforms and long-term support for growing businesses. We began as part of [Tyche Ventures](https://github.com/tycheventures) and became an independent LLP in 2021, led by [Makarand Mane](https://github.com/MakarandMane), a WordPress developer since 2011 and Core contributor.

## What we do

- **Plugin development** — custom WordPress plugins built around your business logic
- **Theme development** — fully custom and child themes (Astra, WPLMS and more)
- **Design, development & customization** — new builds and changes to existing sites
- **WP Doctor** — emergency help: hacked sites, crashes, broken updates
- **Learning Management Systems** — WPLMS and LMS platforms for institutes and trainers
- **Support & maintenance** — updates, backups, security and performance
- **Hire a developer** — dedicated WordPress developer time
- **Domain & hosting** — via our partner brand [FenixHost](https://fenixhost.in/)
